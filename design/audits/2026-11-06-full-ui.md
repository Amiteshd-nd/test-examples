# UX audit — full UI (Vault, editor, style sheet, component page) — 2026-11-06

Skill `plancraft-ux-refine` §2 full audit, run by the docs & UX session after the token system (DS-01) and P2.2a. It builds on the Build session's Vault audit (`2026-11-06-vault-phase14.md`, issues V1–V16) and does not repeat it.

## Summary for the founder (plain language, 5 lines max)
The app is in good shape for keyboard, screen-reader and low-vision users. Nothing overflows at 200 % zoom, focus is always visible, nothing is hover-only, and all text passes contrast except two spots in the folder art.
I fixed nine problems. The biggest: an unnamed Sort dropdown, menus that let focus escape, option switches and tabs that needed one Tab press per option, selected rows shown only by colour, and the drawing breaking in Windows high-contrast mode.
A new automatic check now stops anyone adding raw colours, off-scale spacing or off-scale text sizes.
Three choices are yours (end of this page): the folder-art contrast, a text-size setting for older users, and empty-page wording.

## What passed (automated, light and dark, all 17 screens and states)
- No unnamed controls (after F1). No images without alt text, no duplicate ids. `lang="en"`.
- No horizontal page scroll at 1280 px or at 200 % zoom on the 6 main screens. At 200 % the Vault sidebar stacks above the content.
- Every keyboard stop shows a visible focus change, and no focused item is hidden under a sticky bar. Help and New folder keep focus inside and give it back on Esc. The ⌘K palette is a proper combobox (arrows move, Esc closes).
- Reduced motion: nothing animates. Nothing is revealed only on hover, and no name is cut off without a tooltip.
- **Contrast:** every text colour passes WCAG AA in both themes, except D1 and a disabled button (allowed).
- **Targets:** every control can be hit across at least 24 × 24 px (text links and the switch through an invisible hit area, checked by hand).
- **Spacing:** 78 % of values sit on the 4 px grid; the rest are 2 / 6 px half-steps or documented exceptions (lint). Radii follow the scale; segmented buttons are concentric (4 − 1 px).
