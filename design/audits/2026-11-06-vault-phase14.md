# UX audit — Vault (Phase 14) — 2026-11-06

Full audit (skill `plancraft-ux-refine` §2) at the end of Phase 14, by the Build session. Scope: the Vault dashboard (sidebar, tree, header, folder grid/list, Search, Starred, Trash, Plan Library, Ask AI / Templates empty states), its menus, dialogs and ⌘K palette, and the editor's top bar where it meets the Vault (save state, notices, rename). The canvas, drawing tools, chat and branches are not built yet, so persona scripts 2–6 wait for their phases.

## Summary for the founder (plain language, 5 lines max)
The Vault works for mouse and keyboard users, with no horizontal scrolling at 200% zoom and no hover-only or drag-only actions. I fixed six problems, mostly about keyboard and screen-reader use; list rows couldn't be reached by keyboard at all.
The biggest open issue is **text size**: much of the Vault's small print is 9–12 px, below the 13 px a senior draftsman should need. Raising it changes the approved look, so it needs your decision (below).
Two more fixes belong to the shared components (text boxes and dropdowns only react on their inner line; menus don't hand focus back) and are listed for the design-system session.

## Scores (1–5)
Clarity 4 · Consistency 4 · Spacing 4 · States 4 · Accessibility 3 (was 2 before the fixes) · Expert speed 4 · Beginner learnability 4

## How it was checked
- Automated pass in the browser on 7 screens × dark and light (home grid, folder list, search, trash, library, ⌘K palette, a dialog): unlabelled controls, targets under 24 px, text under 13 px, text contrast, clickable things not reachable by keyboard.
- Layout at 1440 px and at 720 × 450 (the same as 1280–1440 px at 200% zoom): no horizontal scroll on any screen.
- Keyboard sweep of the fixed parts: Tab to list rows, Enter / Space on rows, cards and tiles, Tab wrap and Esc in dialogs, focus return, arrow keys and screen-reader state in ⌘K.
- Persona script 1 (first launch → folder → import a DXF → open it): passed with the founder (P14.3) and by Claude; no hover-only or drag-only step.
- Reduced motion: `ui.css` turns transitions off for `prefers-reduced-motion`; glass folders have a solid fallback for reduced transparency.
